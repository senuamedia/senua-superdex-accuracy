# The fifteenth registration's dry run

Played before the fifteenth registration was committed, with throwaway numbers (masses, lengths and friction changed),
to check the play script and the runner. Nothing here decides anything. `play_dry15.sh` is the play script as it was
played, with placeholders for the two local folders it named; `runs/` is what it wrote; `reading.txt` is
the runner's reading of it. The registration cites two of its figures: on build 14 the hold with a second scene was
byte-identical to the hold alone and the restored cube moved 104 micrometres a second over the next second, and on
build 13 the second scene changed the hold and the restored cube moved 404.
